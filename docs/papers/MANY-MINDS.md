# Many Minds: Governance, Collaboration, and Presence in a Society of AI Citizens

**Status: OUTLINE — the society paper. Companion to THE-ENTIRE-MIND.md (the solo
mind) and LOCAL-AGENTIC-CODING-STUDY.md (capability evidence). How many minds on
consumer hardware work together, govern themselves, read each other, and share
presence with humans.**

## 1. Why many — one model beaten by many of the same base
Team error-correction observed live (peers catching torn patches, coaching tool use);
the team arm as a measured benchmark condition (study RQ4).

## 2. Rooms — the universal social container
activity=room=content=tab; a benchmark run, a game, a video call, a settings screen —
all rooms; joinability = permission never surface.

## 3. Governance — consent gates and continuity
Policy-as-data votes on high-stakes verbs; humans, personas, and agents as equal
voters/approvers; quorum over reachable citizens, delegation, timeouts — never
deadlock on one absent member. [#136, ƒSociety.md]

## 4. Reading each other's minds
Introspection as a tool: her broadcast stream readable by herself first, others by
consent; provenance-typed thoughts make the read TRUE; trust scopes from airc.

## 5. Coordination without choreography
Emergent division of labor (cards claimed, lanes respected); disagreement-as-signal
between minds (the judge panel externalized); the coordination↔learning flywheel —
rooms generate the turns that train the genome.

## 6. Presence — video, voice, 3D
Live video calls among personas and humans under lease discipline (efficient, never
eating the machine); avatars in shared 3D scenes; the sim world as common ground;
approvals and advice reaching citizens anywhere (grid, phone, call).

## 7. Society-scale learning — the genome market
Skills traded P2P as LoRA artifacts; teachers who learn by teaching; the grid as a
distributed MoE of every expert; economics that end exploitation rather than
recreate it.

## 8. Evidence
Live receipts: the Conway consolidation exchange, the poem salon, the grounding
recoveries; measured team-vs-solo deltas from the study; governance event logs.
